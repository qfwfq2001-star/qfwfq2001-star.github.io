SHUQU LIU

Biography

SHUQU LIU (b. 2001, Hubei, China) graduated from China Academy of Art in 2023, studying Fine Arts (Arts Appreciation and Collection).

Drawing on the textures of Nihonga, Liu explores the emotional experience of identity through painting. The practice also encompasses ceramics and photography, including photographs made in Tibet in 2022.

Artist statement

I approach identity as something felt before it can be named. It takes shape in the space between how we see ourselves and how we are seen by others.

Through the textures of Nihonga, I explore surfaces that hold both presence and uncertainty. Layers, interrupted forms and repeated marks become a way of giving these feelings a physical shape.

My paintings leave room for a self that is still forming: at times enclosed, at times exposed, always in relation to the world around it.
